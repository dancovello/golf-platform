# Research: England Top 100 Golf Clubs — Website List & Design Ratings

## Source & scope

- **Source ranking:** https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-courses/britain-ireland/england
- **Scope: England only.** This is not a UK-wide ranking — Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland are separate pages on the same site and are not included here (so St Andrews, Muirfield, Royal Portrush, Royal County Down etc. are absent). Flagged as a possible follow-up in `03-open-questions-and-next-steps.md`.
- **100 ranked entries → 93 unique clubs.** Several clubs have two or three ranked courses that share one official website: Sunningdale (Old & New), Walton Heath (Old & New), The Berkshire (Red & Blue), Wentworth (West & East), Saunton (East & West), and Woburn (Marquess, Duke's & Duchess). Each is listed once below, with all its ranks noted.
- **1 club has no website:** Queenwood Golf Club (#34) is an ultra-private, invite-only club with no public official site found — a useful "zero digital presence" data point in its own right.
- **URL sourcing:** each official site was found via web search and spot-checked to confirm it belongs to that specific club (not a booking aggregator, pro shop, or review site).

## Rating methodology

Each site was reviewed like a product designer would: navigation structure, hero treatment, imagery quality, mobile-responsive signals (viewport meta tag, mobile nav), content depth/organisation, and social proof (testimonials, press quotes, badges). Rated Excellent / Good / Average / Poor / Very Poor. Calibration anchors (client-provided): **Wentworth = Good/Excellent benchmark, Sunningdale = Poor benchmark.**

- **Keep (reference)** — Good or Excellent; worth studying as a design reference.
- **Borderline – spot check** — Average; workable but with real gaps (thin IA, no imagery, stale-feeling).
- **Discard (anti-pattern)** — Poor or Very Poor; illustrates what to avoid, not what to copy.
- **Needs manual recheck** — automated review was blocked (JS-only rendering, SSL error, or robots.txt); could be a modern SPA or a broken site, not yet known which.
- **Not applicable – no site** — Queenwood only.

**Tally:** Excellent 20 · Good 31 · Average 10 · Poor 18 · Very Poor 8 · Needs manual recheck 5 · No website 1 (= 93)

Full per-club rationale, content types found, and UI blocks observed for every row below live in the companion spreadsheet `Top100_England_Golf_Clubs_Websites.xlsx` / `.csv` and in the Clubhouse Blueprint artifact (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3ed00485-8ff1-4c13-8982-46e65edf382f).

## Recurring anti-patterns (what to design out of)

- **Legacy default template** — no custom hero, no imagery, bulleted green fees instead of a table. Seen on well over a dozen sites, most consistently on the `intelligentgolf` platform (Walton Heath, Silloth, West Hill, Hindhead, Ashridge, Royal Wimbledon, Remedy Oak, and more).
- **Stale content** — Camberley Heath's membership page still quoted "2016/2017 season" pricing; Isle of Purbeck's news feed was topped by a 2015 item; Littlestone refers to the 2011 Open as recent.
- **Content gated behind login** — JCB Golf & Country Club hides its own Course and Guest Information pages from visitors who aren't already members.
- **No responsive signal** — no viewport meta tag, no mobile menu (Rye, Broadstone, Seacroft, Royal Worlington & Newmarket, Dunstanburgh Castle).
- **Accordion-as-content** — Royal Wimbledon delivers green fees and etiquette entirely through collapsed accordions instead of a scannable table.
